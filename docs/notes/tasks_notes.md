# Task notes

`src/tasker.c` matches `SLUS_000.07` with C257 `-O2 -G8` and A234.

- The tasker (`src/tasker.c`, `0x80015cb0`..`0x80015f2c`, 636 bytes) is one module. Its names, statics and bodies come from the lineage source Forsaken PC `Tasker.c` (`reference/forsaken_pc`, mapping in `analysis/forsaken_pc/`), which retail reproduces word for word: the `this`/`next` priority walk with the assignment in the `while` condition, the assignment-in-condition pop, a `void` `AddTASKToFree`, and `TaskerInit`'s comma `for` loop with an `int` counter (`int16` adds a sign extension). AT keeps a callback per TASK where Tasker.c keeps a `setjmp` context, splits the pool allocation into `TaskerAlloc` (invented name), passes `TaskCreate` the priority first, and places `TaskDie` before `TaskDispatch`. `TaskMEM`, `TaskFree` and `TaskUsed` are file statics in that order, so `.sbss` (base `0x8008d088` in `config/tasker.json`) puts them at `0x8008d088`/`090`/`098`. The four list helpers are `static` as in Tasker.c (`"static": true` in the map; the three statics are its `static_data`). `g_nextTask` stays external: it fills `TaskCrnt`'s slot in `TaskerInit` but holds the successor TASK.
